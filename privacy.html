Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Who uses the app

The GTA VI Clips app is used only by the owner of GTA VI Clips. Its only purpose is to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. Uploads are made with Clips Kitty. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it stores

The app stores no data about other users. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about anyone.

Credentials

The Google sign-in credentials used to reach the owner's YouTube channel are stored by Clips Kitty on the owner's own PC, encrypted with Windows DPAPI (Data Protection API). They are never sent anywhere except to Google to make API requests.

Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube, you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
